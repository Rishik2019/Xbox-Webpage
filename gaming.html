Xbox Game Pass

Xbox Game Pass is a subscription service that gives members access to a large library of games. The library includes games for Xbox consoles, PC, and cloud gaming depending on the plan.

The Game Pass catalog changes over time as games are added and removed. Xbox currently describes the service as offering hundreds of games, and some plans include games on their release day.

Game Pass can also include benefits such as online console multiplayer, discounts, cloud gaming, and other extras depending on the membership plan.

Game Pass Benefits

  • Hundreds of games
  • Day-one game releases on eligible plans
  • Online console multiplayer on eligible plans
  • Cloud gaming on eligible plans
  • Member discounts and rewards
